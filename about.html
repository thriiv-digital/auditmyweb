About Audit My Web

Audit My Web is a free tool that checks a website's on-page SEO, structured data, and — distinctively — its AI Readiness: the on-page signals that correlate with being cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

How it's different

Unlike our companion site Shrink My Tools, which processes everything entirely in your browser, this tool necessarily fetches the page you enter from our server — browsers don't allow a webpage to directly read another website's content across domains. We fetch only what a normal visitor (or a search engine crawler) would see: the public HTML, robots.txt, and sitemap.xml. Nothing is stored after your report is generated.

What's checked

  • On-Page & Technical — titles, meta tags, headings, indexability, HTTPS, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, and more
  • Structured Data & Social — JSON-LD schema, Open Graph and Twitter Card tags
  • AI Readiness — FAQ schema, AI crawler access (GPTBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, CCBot), llms.txt, and E-E-A-T signals

We're upfront that "AI Readiness" measures controllable, correlated signals — not a guarantee of appearing in any specific AI-generated answer, since no search engine or AI provider publishes an API for that.
