What's Checked

  • On-Page & Technical — title/meta tags, headings, canonical tags, indexability, alt text, HTTPS, mobile viewport, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, URL structure, analytics setup, and more.
  • Structured Data & Social — JSON-LD schema, Open Graph tags, Twitter Card tags.
  • AI Readiness AI — FAQ/Q&A schema, whether AI crawlers (GPTBot, Google-Extended, PerplexityBot, CCBot) are allowed to access the site, llms.txt presence, and E-E-A-T signals. This measures readiness — the on-page signals that correlate with AI citability — not a guarantee of appearing in any specific AI answer, since no public API exposes actual AI Overview citations.
  • Above-the-Fold & Trust Signals — a screenshot of what a visitor sees without scrolling, analyzed for a visible phone number, reviews/ratings, trust badges or certifications, a clear call-to-action, and testimonials.

Everything is fetched and analyzed server-side at the moment you run the audit — nothing is stored, and no account is required.
